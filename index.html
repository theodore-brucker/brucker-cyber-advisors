The Problem

You're Probably Overpaying for Cyber Insurance.

  • Cyber insurance is priced against your security posture. Small businesses average around $130 to $140 a month and mid-size firms pay $400 to $1,250 or more. Underwriters look for specific controls: MFA, endpoint detection, tested backups, and an incident response plan. When they can't verify them, you pay more.
  • Most small and midsize businesses fill out underwriting questionnaires defensively and can't produce evidence for controls they already have. The result is a higher rate at every renewal.
  • Brokers negotiate rates but don't fix your security posture. IT providers deploy tools but don't map them to what your carrier rewards. Nobody owns that gap.
  • Closing it normally means paying a consultant upfront and hoping the savings show up later. Most businesses never make that bet.
  • BCA closes the gap at zero upfront cost. We get paid from the premium reduction we deliver, and nothing else.

Service Offerings

What We Do

Everything below is included in the engagement and funded entirely by your premium savings.

Insurance Policy & Contract Review

A line-by-line review of your policy, exclusions, sublimits, and underwriting requirements. We flag coverage gaps, requirements you may be out of compliance with, and the terms that drive your rate.

Underwriting Gap Assessment

An evaluation of your security posture against the criteria your carrier underwrites. You get a findings report ordered by premium impact, not generic best practice.

Control Implementation

Engineering work to close the gaps: MFA rollout, endpoint detection and response, backup hardening and testing, logging and monitoring, and incident response planning.

Evidence Collection & Attestation

We build the documentation your broker and carrier need to verify every control: policies, configurations, test results, and attestations.
