Lower your cyber insurance premium. Pay nothing unless you save.

Brucker Cyber Advisors reviews your cyber insurance policy and identifies the gaps in your security controls driving up your premiums. We then help you build the evidence your carrier needs to re-price your risk.

Our fee comes out of your first-year savings. If your premium doesn't drop, you owe us nothing.

How it works
  1. Policy & contract review We analyze your current cyber insurance policy, underwriting questionnaire, and renewal terms to find what your carrier prices against and where you're being penalized.
  2. Control identification We map the gap between your current security posture and the controls most likely to qualify you for a lower premium.
  3. Implementation & evidence collection We guide the implementation of any deficient security controls, and assemble the documented evidence your carrier needs to re-price your risk at renewal.
  4. Savings You renew your policy with the newly implemented controls and documented evidence in hand, and your premium drops. The security improvements are yours to keep.
