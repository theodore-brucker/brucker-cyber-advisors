Lower your cyber insurance premium. Pay nothing unless you save.

Brucker Cyber Advisors reviews your cyber insurance policy and identifies the gaps in your security controls driving up your premiums. We then help you build the evidence your carrier needs to re-price your risk.

Our fee comes out of your first-year savings. If your premium doesn't drop, you owe us nothing.

The Process

  1. Policy & Contract Review - We analyze your current cyber insurance policy, underwriting questionnaire, and renewal terms to find what your carrier prices against and where you're being penalized.
  2. Gap Identification - We map the gap between your current technology environment and the security controls most likely to qualify you for a lower premium.
  3. Gap Remediation - We guide you through the implementation of any deficient security controls, from multi-factor authentication and endpoint detection to backup hardening and incident response planning.
  4. Evidence Collection - We assemble documentation behind each remediated control, including policies and configuration records, presenting your underwriter with the evidence they need to re-price your risk.
  5. Savings - You renew your policy with the newly implemented controls and documented evidence in hand, and your premium drops.
