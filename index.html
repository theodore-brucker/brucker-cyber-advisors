The Problem

You're Probably Overpaying for Cyber Insurance.

  • Most small and midsize businesses fill out underwriting questionnaires defensively and can't produce evidence for controls they already have. The result is a higher rate at every renewal.
How It Works

One Engagement, Four Steps.

1

Policy & Contract Review

We analyze your current cyber insurance policy, underwriting questionnaire, and renewal terms to find what your carrier prices against and where you're being penalized.

2

Control Identification

We map the gap between your current security posture and the controls that qualify you for a lower premium, ordered by premium impact, not generic best practice.

3

Implementation & Evidence

We guide implementation of the missing controls, from MFA and endpoint detection to backup hardening and incident response planning, and assemble the evidence your carrier needs to reprice your risk at renewal.

4

Savings

Your premium drops, and the security improvements are yours to keep.
